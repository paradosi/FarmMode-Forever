# Changelog

## v1.1.0
- **Minimap button**: left-click switches farm mode on or off, right-click opens the settings, drag it around the edge. Turn it off with *Show the minimap button* in the settings or `/farm minimap`. With *Hide minimap buttons* on, it hides with the others while farming
- Coordinates are no longer drawn twice with Polestar's minimap coordinates on: farm mode hides Polestar's while it shows its own, and gives them back after

## v1.0.0
- First release: FarmMode rebuilt for WoW Forever
- The minimap is resized rather than scaled, so it shows more ground while node and quest icons stay their normal size
- Round or square shape, with or without a border
- Size, zoom, opacity and position, all applied live while farm mode is on
- In combat the map can close (and come back after), fade, or stay; a toggle pressed during combat waits for it to end
- Clicks go through the map to the world while farming; hold Alt to drag, ping or zoom it
- Remembers being on across reloads and logins, can turn on when you mount up, and hides in dungeons, battlegrounds, cities and inns until you leave (all optional)
- Coordinates under the map
- Optional rotating map while farming
- Optional alternating herb and ore tracking for herbalist-miners
- Hiding minimap buttons also hides other addons' minimap buttons
- Addon compartment entry: left-click toggles, right-click opens the options
- Settings window in the wowdb.dev look (navy, gold, Oswald and JetBrains Mono), matching Forever Alts and Nightclaw Ears
- The settings window shows the version and has a **Help and bug reports** button with the GitHub address to copy; `/farm help` prints both
